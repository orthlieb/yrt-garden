# 6. Practical Integration: Economy, Access, and Game-Ready Abstractions

This section translates the physical and systems-level properties of Manite™ (mana) into practical considerations for economics, access, and rules abstractions suitable for narrative and tabletop use (e.g., Ironsworn-style frameworks).

---

## 6.1 Economic Realities and Access

### 6.1.1 Manufacturing Cost and Scale

Real-world analyses of nanomaterials and nanorobotics emphasize that:

- Nanomaterial production is capital-intensive, with costs driven by high-precision equipment, stringent quality control, and regulatory overhead.
- Even relatively simple magnetic nanoparticles can cost on the order of hundreds of dollars per kilogram at industrial scale, with functionalized or medical-grade variants being much more expensive.
- The nanorobotics market is projected to be worth tens of billions of dollars globally, with growth constrained by high R&D and manufacturing costs.

By analogy, Manite production:

- Requires sophisticated nanomanufacturing infrastructure (e.g., electrochemical nanoimprinting, top-down lithography, or bottom-up self-assembly with tight process control).
- Involves multiple functional layers (photovoltaic, actuation, control, effector modules) and color-specific chemistries.
- Faces intense regulatory and testing burdens, particularly for Azure/Amber (medical/BCI), further inflating costs.

### 6.1.2 In-World Price and Scarcity

Consequently:

- **Gray** is the cheapest and most widespread, especially in construction and consumer goods.
- **Verdant** is moderately priced and common in agricultural regions but less so in dense urban centers.
- **Crimson** and **Luminous** are significantly more expensive due to energy density and safety-critical roles.
- **Azure** and especially **Amber** are highly regulated and costly, largely confined to licensed medical and guild/military users.
- **Black** is black-market or state-secret only; its possession is typically criminalized.

Narratively, manite is:

- Valuable and unevenly distributed—richer polities and guilds monopolize advanced colors.
- A driver of inequality and conflict—control over Azure/Amber/Crimson access translates into health, power, and military advantage.

## 6.2 Abstracting Manite as Tracks and Assets (Ironsworn-Style)

### 6.2.1 Mapping to Core Tracks

Ironsworn’s core tracks (Health, Spirit, Supply, Momentum) abstract complex resource and condition states. Manite can be overlaid as:

- **Health:**
  - Augmented and protected by Azure, Gray, Luminous. Azure manite can provide bonus healing, mitigations, or narrative justification for surviving severe harm.

- **Spirit:**
  - Influenced by Amber and Azure (courage, pain modulation, sensory clarity), but also threatened by Black and Amber misuse (hallucinations, manipulation).

- **Supply:**
  - General manite availability (vials, cartridges, coatings, charges) can be folded into Supply: resupplying in a settlement may explicitly include replenishing or recharging manite stocks.

- **Momentum:**
  - Successful, clever use of manite—deploying the right color at the right time—naturally fuels Momentum gains (advantage, opportunity, initiative).

### 6.2.2 Dedicated Manite Resource Tracks

For settings that focus on manite, additional tracks can be introduced:

- **Gray Integrity (0–5):**
  - Represents how much functional Gray armor/structure you have; drops when structures are shattered, corroded, or set permanently.

- **Crimson Charge (0–5):**
  - Overall energy state of your Crimson reserves.
  - Falls when you boost other colors or create overdriven effects; recovers slowly via solar/electrical recharge or resupply.

- **Azure Load (0–3):**
  - Represents available Azure vials or equivalent therapeutic capacity.
  - Spent when performing major healing or long-duration enhancements.

- **Amber Stability (0–5):**
  - Health and reliability of your BCI and control stack.
  - Falls on severe shocks, EMP, corruption, or hacking attempts; low values can impose penalties or hallucinations.

Optional tracks:

- **Luminous Buffer:** how much shielding/locking capacity remains before your wards falter.
- **Corruption / Black Exposure:** a rising track which, when filled, indicates serious systemic contamination or personal corruption.

## 6.3 Manite as Assets, Moves, and Companions

### 6.3.1 Color-Specific Assets

You can model each color as one or more Ironsworn assets:

- **Gray Manite Armor (Combat Talent or Companion):**
  - Grants bonuses to Face Danger and Endure Harm when the armor is active.
  - Abilities to reshuffle configuration mid-scene (e.g., trade protection for mobility).

- **Azure Bioforge (Path or Ritual):**
  - Allows enhanced Heal moves and temporary boosts to Edge/Iron/other stats, with clear risk of backlash on a miss (muscle tears, neural overload).

- **Crimson Battery (Companion or Ritual):**
  - Lets you “overcharge” other assets or moves for additional effect by spending Crimson Charge or suffering lasting depletion.

- **Verdant Engine (Path or Ritual):**
  - Provides narrative authority to dramatically alter local flora, restore devastated ground, or choke out an area with overgrowth.

- **Luminous Ward (Companion or Ritual):**
  - Mitigates environmental and incoming harm (heat, radiation, projectiles); can lock other manite states.

- **Amber Interface (Path):**
  - Enables you to direct manite at range, perceive through it, and resist or suffer mental strain in high-stakes control attempts.

- **Black Contagion (Forbidden Path/Ritual):**
  - Potent but extremely dangerous abilities for decay, reanimation, and corruption; always comes with dire narrative costs.

### 6.3.2 Manite-Infused Moves

Examples of custom moves:

- **Invoke Crimson Overdrive:**
  - When you unleash Crimson to gain impossible speed, strength, or thrust, roll +Iron or +Heart (depending on whether this is raw will or trained control).
  - On a strong hit: achieve a spectacular effect and mark -1 Crimson Charge.
  - On a weak hit: you achieve it, but choose 1–2 complications (heat damage, structural damage to Gray, Azure backlash, etc.).

- **Set the Gray:**
  - When you command Gray to fuse the environment into a permanent structure, roll +Wits.
  - On a strong hit: create a durable, lasting feature that benefits you. On a weak hit: it works, but you lose a significant fraction of your mobile Gray and invite attention or unintended consequences.

- **Azure Surge:**
  - When you burn Azure reserves to save a life or push your body past safe limits, roll +Iron (endure) or +Heart (care).
  - On a hit: clear harm or gain a powerful but temporary edge; on a miss: mark debilities or corruption.

These moves tie directly to the physical and systemic properties of the manite described in earlier sections.

## 6.4 Resupply, Crafting, and Repair

### 6.4.1 Resupply in Settlements

Given the high capital cost of manite production:

- Major settlements, guild halls, and corporate facilities are primary sources for **fresh vials, cartridges, and coatings**.
- Resupply may require favors, quests, or substantial in-world currency, reflecting market reports that emphasize high costs and slow scaling of nanorobotics and advanced nanomaterials.

In Ironsworn terms, a Resupply-equivalent move in a settlement can explicitly:

- Refill Crimson Charge, restore Gray Integrity, replenish Azure Load.
- Offer opportunities to upgrade or modify manite assets at narrative and mechanical cost.

### 6.4.2 Field Repairs and Improvised Fabrication

In the field:

- **Gray + Crimson** can be combined to patch or improvise tools, barricades, shelters.
- **Azure** can restore partial functionality to damaged implants.
- **Verdant** can help grow living materials (wood, root systems) that Gray then reinforces.

However:

- Field repairs tend to be less efficient and may introduce impurities or instabilities.
- Repeated field-bootstrapped manite structures are more likely to fail under stress or be susceptible to corruption and environmental attack.

## 6.5 Positioning Manite in Narrative Play

### 6.5.1 Tone and Themes

Manite introduces strong thematic elements:

- **Miraculous capability vs. catastrophic risk:**
  - Azure/Amber allow near-miraculous healing and enhancement; Black and large-scale failures threaten ecophagy and collapse.

- **Inequality and control:**
  - Access to high-grade manite is uneven, mirroring current concerns about advanced nanotech markets and infrastructure.

- **Infrastructure as character:**
  - Gray, Verdant, Luminous, and Crimson turn the environment itself into an active, responsive entity—terrain is no longer static.

### 6.5.2 Guidance for GMs and Players

To keep manite grounded and coherent:

- Treat each color as a **tool with clear constraints**, not a universal solution.
- Let **energy, environment, and governance** matter; not every situation allows unfettered manite use.
- Use failures and edge cases (corruption, depletion, misfires) as story hooks, not just penalties.

For an Ironsworn hack:

- Start with a **modest set of manite assets** and a few color-specific moves.
- Slowly introduce more exotic colors (Luminous, Black) and complex interactions as the campaign escalates.
- Use vows and progress tracks to represent major manite-related projects (cleansing a Gray Zone, stabilizing an Amber network, founding a Verdant-supported settlement).

This approach preserves the hard-science flavor and systemic tension outlined in earlier sections while supporting rich emergent storytelling.
