title: Government

Freeport is ruled by a triumvate of leaders: the lord of merchants, chief priest of the Puritans, and archmage of the Conclave. A shadow lord, the chief of assassins, is the unacknowledged silent fourth in this power quadrangle, although the public only openly speaks of three.
