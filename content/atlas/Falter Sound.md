# Falter Sound

*Boundary · Nysis / Buralia / Freeport · The salt arm; the only road that costs nothing · off-oracle*

**Region:** Falter Sound · **Location:** Bay/Fjord
**Location detail:** The salt water running north from Freeport's harbour to where it widens into the Sea of Bees

The Sound is the reason there is anything here to write about. It runs north from [[Freeport]] for a hundred and sixty miles of open salt water, deep the whole way, and comes out at the top into the [[Sea of Bees]] on the shoulder of [[Piscis]]. Everything the region sells to anybody beyond it goes down this water, and almost none of it goes any other way.

It is deep because it was drowned rather than dug. Whatever the valley was before the sea came up it, it is now forty miles across at the widest and takes a loaded hull anywhere along its length, which is the fact the whole regional economy is built on and which no other water in four hundred miles can match. Ostrea has the only rival for depth and sits at the end of the road. Freeport has depth and the road both.

## The Two Shores

They are not the same country and they are not the same century.

The **western shore** is Nysis, and going north from Freeport it is [[Nebula Forest|forest]], then the blight. [[Ravenna]] comes down to the water for eleven miles and the road that skirts it runs the fringe the whole way, which is why almost nothing uses the road and everything uses the Sound. Past the blight the shore is [[Verday Groves|Verdani]] ground, then the cut, then [[Piscis]].

The **eastern shore** is Buralian on paper and nobody's in practice: dry scrub for most of its length, [[Spero]] behind its wall in the middle of it, and at the head of the Sound the [[Weirden Fen|Weirden]] delta with [[Alneta]] on it. Thirty miles of channel and four hundred fen-folk, and past that nothing anybody will admit to until the mission.

The distance between the two is forty miles at the narrowest useful crossing, which puts the region's two richest blights in sight of each other on a clear day. Men at Ravenna say they can see [[Spero]] and cannot, and say it anyway.

## What Moves On It

Everything. Refined mana goes south under escort and never by sea, which is the one exception and is a Conclave rule rather than a nautical one. Everything else floats: salt fish and oyster down from [[Ostrea]] and Piscis, alder piling and fen goods down from Alneta, timber, iron, grain, cloth and every manufactured thing back up.

There is no toll on the Sound. Nobody owns the water and nobody has ever worked out how they would. What Freeport owns is the canal at the bottom of it, where the water narrows to sixty-five feet and a hull cannot go round. Everything that comes through is landed, opened, valued off its manifest and assessed, and then charged again for the chit, the inspection, the flag code and the chain. Owning one gate on a hundred and sixty miles of free water has always been enough.

The Sound is also where a cargo goes when it does not want the [[Termin]] tariff, which is most cargo. Anything valuable enough to be worth freight leaves Nysis by water, is landed at Freeport, is carried across onto a Bay barge and taken up the canal, pays duty on its declared value and a bill at each end of the handling, and still arrives cheaper and sooner than it would have crossed a border a hundred and forty miles closer. That difference is the size of the tariff at Termin, and the factors on both sides of it can do the sum in their heads.

## Weather, and the Quiet Sea

It is a sheltered water and it is not a safe one. The Sound funnels: a northerly that is a stiff breeze in the Sea of Bees arrives here compressed between two shores with nowhere to spread, and the chop it raises is short, steep and much worse than the wind that made it. Every skipper on this water can tell you which reaches to be out of by afternoon in which season, and the ones who cannot are working somebody else's boat.

Marine insurance at Freeport is written against the Sound and against the Sea of Bees at different rates, and the difference is the whole of the underwriting trade. **228 SU** is still called the **Year of the Quiet Sea** in the counting houses: a season of unbroken calm in which premiums came down, four houses wrote thin, and the following winter took most of what they had made. Nobody has repeated the mistake and everybody is fairly sure somebody will.

**Features**
- Deep salt water the whole hundred and sixty miles, and a shore you can lose sight of
- The blight coming down to the western waterline, and the road nobody uses beside it
- Two blights on two shores, forty miles apart, in sight on a clear day
- Fen delta at the head, and channels that moved since the last survey
- Convoys of refined mana on the road instead, under escort, because the order will not ship it
- Short steep chop out of nothing, in a wind that was mild an hour north
- Coasters riding low up the coast and light coming back
- Freeport's chain at the bottom of it, and the hours it is down
- Insurance rates quoted by reach and by season

**Quest Starter**
> A hull has been found drifting in the middle of the Sound with nobody aboard, no damage, and the boat's papers in order in a locked box. It is one of the fen coasters off the eastern run. Freeport's underwriters want it declared a loss and the matter closed inside a fortnight, the fen skippers want it left exactly where it is until somebody says a name, and one of the four houses has already paid out on a cargo that was never loaded.
