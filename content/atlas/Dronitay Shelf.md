# Dronitay Shelf

*Remote · Nysis / Mososi · The drowned shelf; salvage*

**Region:** Dronitay Shelf · **Location:** Lake
**Location detail:** A drowned valley south of the Suda Forest, fed by every stream off the southern Pinna

The Dronitay is a lake that does not behave like one. The Unmade impounded a broad shallow valley south of the [[Suda Forest]], and two hundred and thirty years later the water is still there: fifty-odd square miles of it, fed by every stream coming off the southern Pinna and the Suda country, and spilling over its southern rim in a broad shallow sheet that gathers into channels and goes down to Falter Bay. What makes it strange is the floor. It is dead level, engineered level, and the whole sheet is nowhere more than waist to chest deep. You can stand up anywhere on the Dronitay. That is the first thing anyone tells you about it and it is not the reassurance it sounds like.

Because it is shallow and flat and open, the wind owns it. A day of hard southerly will pile a foot of water against the far rim and take a foot off the near one, and the margins move by hundreds of yards between morning and evening: ground that was dry at dawn is knee-deep by noon and the skiff you left is gone. Stirred water goes the colour of milky tea and stays that way for a day; on a still morning it goes clear and you can see the whole bottom, the streets, the field walls, the shapes of things that do not move. Reed beds stand in the shallows at the edges. Herons and waders work them in flocks that lift all at once for no reason anyone can see.

The town is still in the middle. Whatever the Unmade called the place they flooded is standing there in three or four feet of water: walls to chest height, doorways submerged, upper storeys and chimney stacks and one squared tower breaking the surface, everything below silted to the sills. It is the richest salvage ground in the region and worked by very few people, because getting to it means poling a flat-bottomed skiff for hours across open water with no cover in any direction, and because of what shares the water. The salvagers do not agree on what those are and do not much like discussing it. What they agree on is the method: you go in the dry season, you go at first light before the wind gets up, you work from the skiff, and whatever you are reaching for, you do not get out and feel for it with your feet.

**Features**
- Fifty square miles of standing water, dead level, chest deep at most
- The old shoreline visible all round as a pale line at eye height
- Wind moving the whole sheet, and the margins with it
- Water like milky tea for a day after anything stirs it
- On a still morning, the entire bottom visible: streets, walls, shapes
- The drowned town midwater: wall-tops, upper storeys, one squared tower
- Flat-bottomed skiffs, poled, and no cover anywhere for any of them
- Reed beds and wading birds along the margins
- Small craft still lying where they settled, silted to the gunwales
- The southern rim, where the whole sheet tips over and goes to the bay

**Quest Starter**
> A salvage crew has come back from the tower at the town's centre with something they will not show anyone and will not put back. Two of the four are already sick in a way the Azure Mendicants have not seen. The other two have gone out again. The dry season has three weeks left in it before the water comes up.
