# Fluenti

*City · Nysis · **Settled** · Timber, sawmilling, river driving · pop. ~2,000*

**Exports** — Sawn timber in every dimension the region uses: ship frames and spars for Freeport, pit props for the Pinna, roof beams, planking, shingles, and barrel staves by the thousand for the coopering towns down the coast. Fluenti sells structure.

**Imports** — Grain, iron and edge tools, rope and canvas for the drives, salt fish, cloth. A timber city makes one thing superbly and nothing else at all.

Fluenti stands where the river comes down out of the [[Profunda Forest]] and gives itself to Lake Mater, and it is named for the water because the water is the business. Everything Fluenti sells arrives floating. The cut is made miles upstream and inland, and the logs come down on the spring rise in a drive that takes days, closes the river to everything else, and is watched by the whole city from the banks like a festival — which it partly is, and partly a count of who came back.

The mills are strung along the outflow, undershot wheels and long saws, and the sound of them carries across the water so that Materton can hear Fluenti working on a still day thirty miles off. Below the mills, booms hold sorted timber in floating pens by owner's mark until a barge takes it. From there it goes south down the lake — and the lake is where the arrangement gets interesting, because Lake Mater is a border. Fluenti is Nysis; [[Termin]] and [[Materton]] on the far shore are Mososi, and the tariff the two countries maintain applies to a barge exactly as it applies to a wagon. In principle duty is collected at the receiving quay. In practice a lake is a great deal harder to hold than a road, the shoreline is long, and a barge that never quite lands at a customs quay has not, technically, arrived. Fluenti's respectable timber trade and Fluenti's other timber trade use the same booms and largely the same men.

It is the most northerly settled place on the lake, and the Profunda begins where the cut ends, which gives Fluenti a nervousness the size of it does not otherwise explain. River-drivers come back from the upper reaches with a habit of not finishing sentences about what is up there. The cut is pushed further every decade, the drives get longer, and the men who run them get more expensive. Nobody in Fluenti calls this a problem. They call it the price of the timber.

The obvious thought — that Fluenti has forest and [[Collima]] is running out of it — has occurred to everyone and works for nobody. Charcoal for the Pinna smelters has to be burned near where it is used, because charcoal is light for its bulk and hauling it any distance costs more heat than it delivers. A hundred and forty miles of lake and mountain road between the Profunda and the Empire Mine is not a supply route, it is an argument for letting Collima finish its own forest. Fluenti sells timber, not fuel, and is content to.

The city is run by an urbestro like every Nysisian town, and like every Nysisian urbestro he draws a share of the Conclave's rent for [[Ravenna]] each year, which in Fluenti's case funds a watch that is notably uninterested in what leaves the booms after dark.

**Features**
- The spring drive, and the whole city on the banks to watch it
- Log booms in floating pens, sorted by owner's mark
- Undershot wheels and the long saws going
- Sawdust and pitch, in everything, permanently
- River-drivers with caulked boots and no small talk
- Barges loading at dusk for the far shore
- Green timber stacked in drying yards for years
- The forest wall upstream, and the cut biting into it
- Lake fog rolling in and taking the far bank
- Talk that stops when someone asks about the upper reaches

**Quest Starter**
> This year's drive has jammed eleven miles upstream, at the deepest point the cut has ever reached, and the crew sent to break it has not come back. The rise is falling. If the jam is not broken in days the whole year's timber is stranded until next spring — and Fluenti's creditors, several of whom are owed in Mososi coin for cargoes that were never declared, will not wait a year.
