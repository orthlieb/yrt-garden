# Sea of Bees

*Remote · nobody's · The open water; the only road out of the region · off-oracle*

**Region:** Sea of Bees · **Location:** Sea
**Location detail:** The open sea north and east of the coast, from Piscis round past Ostrea to country nobody charts

North of where [[Falter Sound]] widens out there is no far shore. The Sea of Bees runs east and north past anything anyone in this region has business with, and what is on the other side of it is a matter of opinion held by people who have not been there. It is the one road out that nobody built, nobody owns, and nobody can close.

[[Piscis]] sits at the corner where the Sound opens into it. [[Ostrea]] is sixty miles up the coast on the last shelter it offers, and past Ostrea the shore turns north and stops being anybody's. Between them the Nether Strand runs eleven miles of grey tidal flat, and that flat, and the banks off it, are what the two cities live on.

## Why It Is Called That

Nobody knows, and the answer people give is probably wrong.

What they tell you is that in a settled easterly the shoal water off the Strand hums, low and steady, felt through a hull before it is heard, and that this is the sea working over the banks. That much is true and any Piscis boat can find it for you. That it is where the name comes from is a folk etymology of exactly the kind that survives because it is satisfying, and the name is older than any of the towns using it. [[Language|What survived the Unmaking]] survived without its reasons attached, and this is one of the words that came through with the meaning stripped off it.

## The Banks

The reason there is a fishing fleet is that the cold water turns here.

Somewhere off the Strand a cold current comes down and meets what is coming up, and where it turns it lifts everything from the bottom into the light, and the fish are there because the feed is there and has been for longer than anyone has been counting. The Piscis fleet works it before dawn in long open boats under tan sails. Ostrea works the flats and the leased ground inshore of it. Between them they feed a third of the region's year, because the [[Religion|Ecclesia]] calendar puts a third of the year on abstinence and salt fish is what most of three countries actually eats on those days.

The banks were richer than either side could exhaust for most of two centuries. That has stopped being true, and the other side is the [[Merrow|merrow]], who have fished the same ground with nets of their own for longer than anybody has been keeping a record and who have responded to a fleet going further out and staying longer exactly the way anyone would. See [[Piscis]] for what that looks like from a deck.

## The Deep Water

Beyond the banks the bottom drops and stays down, and this is where the region touches the rest of the world.

Deep-draft traders come in off it for Freeport, which is the only harbour on this coast that will take them without lightering, and Ostrea, which will take them and then leave them at the end of the road. Ostrea's whalers go the other way: four to eight weeks out to the northern grounds, thirty men to a hull, out of a town with perhaps four hundred grown ones, so that when the ships are out a quarter of the city is out with them.

Weather arrives with about two days' warning and no more, off water nobody is standing on. The insurance houses at Freeport write the Sea of Bees at a different rate from the Sound and always have.

This is Remote ground and it is worth being exact about why, because there is more traffic on it than on any road in the Atlas. The grade is a statement about support. A wagon that stops on the Termin road is a nuisance. A hull that stops out here is on its own, and everybody aboard knows the arithmetic before they cast off.

**Features**
- No far shore in any direction that matters
- The hum off the shoal water in a settled easterly, felt before it is heard
- Cold water turning over the banks, and the gull-clouds that mark it
- Tan sails standing out before first light, and the same boats back by noon
- Deep-draft hulls riding at anchor off Piscis, waiting on lighters
- Whalers gone eight weeks, and a quarter of Ostrea gone with them
- Merrow nets in deep water, and cut lines coming up
- Weather with two days' warning and nowhere to put in
- North of Ostrea, a coast that is on no chart anybody will sell you

**Quest Starter**
> A deep-draft trader has come in to Ostrea off the northern water with a cargo nobody ordered, a crew who will not say which port they cleared, and a chart aboard showing eleven days of coastline north of anything the harbourmaster has ever seen drawn. The urbestro wants the chart. The Conclave has offered for the chart. The master says the chart is not for sale and that he is going back, and he is provisioning for four months.
