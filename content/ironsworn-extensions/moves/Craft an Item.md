# Craft an Item

*Move · Yrt*

*When you use your skill to craft an item.*

When you **use your skill to craft an item**, draw on your training and roll. If you act…
- With expertise, insight, and observation: Roll **+wits**.
- With deception, stealth, or trickery: Roll **+shadow**.

**Strong Hit.** You perfectly craft your item. Consume −1 supply. Take +1 momentum.

**Weak Hit.** You craft your item but with complications. Consume −1 supply. Choose one:
- The item requires more materials than you thought. Consume an additional −1 supply.
- The item is weaker or more fragile than intended.
- The item has a telltale flaw.

**Miss.** You bungle the operation and the item is ruined. Consume −1 supply. Pay the Price.
