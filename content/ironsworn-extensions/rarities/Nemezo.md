# Nemezo

*Rarity · 5 XP*

A double-edged dagger forged from the steel of a Conclave foreman's crown, with a small amber inset at the pommel. The blade alone is ordinary steel; the danger lives in the sheath — a plain leather exterior over a luminous-lined interior holding a small reservoir of volatile Black mana. Drawn from the sheath, the lower inches of the blade carry a thin necrotic coat. Wounds dealt by Nemezo refuse to heal: flesh blackens around the entry, the corruption spreads, and the dying is unhurried. Treat Heal moves as -1 against harm dealt by this dagger until the Black is cleansed.
