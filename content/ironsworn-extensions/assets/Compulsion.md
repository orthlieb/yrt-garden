# Compulsion

*Asset · Ritual*

*Influence thoughts, emotions, and perceptions in a target's mind.*

This ritual lets a caster influence thoughts, emotions, and perceptions in a target’s mind. It is more dangerous than simple illusions, because it acts inside the mind rather than in the air. Amber mana interfaces tightly with the nervous system and sensory processing. It can project illusions, dampen fear or pain, or induce specific emotional states; extended or forceful use risks addiction, mindbreak, or erosion of identity.

**Risks:** Repeated use on the same mind within a short period increases Resistance by +1 each time (max 2) and may create fragmented or contradictory memories. If you roll a miss on a compulsion with Depth 2 or Scope 2, mark a debility related to mental strain (sleeplessness, intrusive memories, emotional numbness). Unsanctioned deep control, if discovered by the Conclave, carries severe consequences.

## Abilities

1. **Compulsion.** You can cast compulsion spells to influence thoughts, emotions, and perceptions. Decide the effect you want, record the total difficulty by summing the difficulty factors, and [[Cast Conclave Ritual|Cast Conclave Ritual]]. You must spend at least 1 mana.
   *Moves: [[Cast Conclave Ritual]]*

2. **Adept Compulsion.** As above, but the spell difficulty is reduced by 2 (minimum 0) given mastery of the compulsion skill.

3. **Master Compulsion.** As above, but the spell difficulty is reduced by 4 instead of 2 (minimum 0).

## Difficulty Factors

| Factor | 1 | 2 | 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Depth** | Surface nudge — momentary distraction, fleeting doubt, slight calm. | Emotional steering — fear, trust, guilt, confidence for minutes. | Directive compulsion — you plant a specific urge or instruction. |
| **Clarity** | Vague impression — a feeling they can second-guess. | Convincing emotion or thought — they think it is their own. | Overriding priority — it dominates other concerns while it lasts. |
| **Scope** | Single emotional axis — fear, calm, curiosity, etc. | Emotion plus a simple belief (“you can trust me,” “they are watching”). | Emotion plus a specific, framed memory or interpretation. |
| **Duration** | Seconds — enough to slip a question past their guard. | Minutes. | Up to an hour or until jolted (pain, shock, another caster). |
| **Resistance** | Willing / unsuspecting — they want to talk, or think you are helping. | Guarded / anxious — afraid, suspicious, but not trained. | Hardened — Brotherhood-trained, fanatic, or currently terrified. |
| **Subtlety** | Crude push — they feel pushed, may notice something is off. | Gentle guidance — they rationalize it as their own reaction. | Seamless masking — memories fill in naturally; they are sure it was all their idea. |
