# Touched, Varanine

*Asset · Touched*

*Varanine-touched: a patient ambush and a killing rush, a tongue that reads blood on the wind, and bony scale beneath the skin that turns a blow.*

You have been touched by YRT and exhibit animal traits. Roll on the [[Character - Touched|YRT – Touched]] oracle to determine the extent and conditions of your nature.

## Abilities

1. **Ambush Predator.** You wait without moving, then cross the ground in a heartbeat. When you Secure an Advantage by lying in wait and rushing prey from ambush, add +1, and you may roll +iron (even if another stat would normally apply). On a strong hit, take +2 momentum, or seize the initiative outright.
   *Moves: Secure An Advantage*

2. **Bloodscent.** You taste the air and read it. When you Gather Information to track wounded prey, carrion, blood, or a quarry that passed this way, add +1, and you may roll +wits (even if another stat would normally apply). On a strong hit, you learn not just where they went, but how hurt, how afraid, and how far ahead.
   *Moves: Gather Information*

3. **Osteoderm.** Bony scales lie beneath your skin. When you Endure Harm, add +1. On a strong hit, take +2 momentum as the blow skids off the armor beneath.
   *Moves: Endure Harm*
