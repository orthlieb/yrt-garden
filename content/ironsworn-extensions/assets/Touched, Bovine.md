# Touched, Bovine

*Asset · Touched*

*Bovine-touched: an unstoppable charge, an unbreakable will, and a voice that bends a room to silence.*

You have been touched by YRT and exhibit animal traits. Roll on the [[Character - Touched|YRT – Touched]] oracle to determine the extent and conditions of your nature.

## Abilities

1. **Gore.** Your bulk and lowered horns turn forward motion into a shattering blow. When you Strike or Clash by charging — at speed, with armored shoulder, lowered horn, or full body weight — you may roll +iron (even if another stat would normally apply). On a strong hit, also deal +1 harm and your foe is thrown off-balance, knocked back, or driven from their footing.
   *Moves: Strike, Clash*

2. **Steady Nerve.** Your nerve does not break easily. When you Endure Stress, add +1. On a strong hit, take +2 momentum instead of +1 as your unflappable temper carries you through.
   *Moves: Endure Stress*

3. **Bellow.** Your lowing call settles arguments before they begin. When you Compel by sheer force of voice or presence — a bellow, a quiet steady refusal, a rumbling commandment — add +1, and you may roll +iron (even if another stat would normally apply). On a strong hit, also take +1 momentum.
   *Moves: Compel*
