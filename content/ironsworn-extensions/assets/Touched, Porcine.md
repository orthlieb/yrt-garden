# Touched, Porcine

*Asset · Touched*

*Porcine-touched: unerring nose for hidden threats, iron constitution, and an instinct for finding sustenance.*

You have been touched by YRT and exhibit animal traits. Roll on the [[Character - Touched|YRT – Touched]] oracle to determine the extent and conditions of your nature.

## Abilities

1. **Keen Nose.** You have a powerful sense of smell. When you Gather Information to detect hidden threats, ambushes, or track a scent, add +1. On a strong hit, also take +1 momentum as your snout allows you to follow the scent to greater clues.
   *Moves: Gather Information*

2. **Thick Hide.** Your thick hide and dense body absorb punishment others cannot weather. When you Endure Harm, add +1. On a strong hit, take +2 momentum instead of +1 as your stubborn constitution shrugs off the blow.
   *Moves: Endure Harm*

3. **Rooting.** Your rooting instinct finds sustenance where others see only wasteland. When you Resupply in the wilderness or desperate conditions, add +1. On a strong hit, take +2 supply instead of +1.
   *Moves: Resupply*
