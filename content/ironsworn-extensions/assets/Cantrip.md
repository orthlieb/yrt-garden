# Cantrip

*Asset · Ritual*

*Minor magical tricks: clean, light, warm, lock, and more.*

You have learned minor magical tricks — small, reliable effects drawn from mana seeds. Choose 2 cantrips when you acquire this asset.

**Limitations:** A cantrip cannot affect anything larger than you can hold in two hands, cannot last longer than 10 minutes or until dispelled (see level exceptions below), cannot reach farther than 10 paces (line of sight required), and cannot directly inflict harm in combat. Most cantrips take several seconds to cast, up to a few minutes for larger or more complex work.

## Abilities

1. **Cantrips.** When you perform a cantrip, [[Cast Conclave Ritual|Cast Conclave Ritual]] with a base difficulty of 1. Add +1 if cast at range, and +1 if cast under stress (e.g. in combat or while being watched). On a **strong hit**, the cantrip works as intended; take +1 momentum. On a **weak hit**, the cantrip works but choose one: it is weaker or shorter than intended, it draws unwanted attention, or you suffer -1 spirit from the strain. On a **miss**, the cantrip misfires; Pay the Price.
   *Moves: [[Cast Conclave Ritual]], Pay The Price*

2. **Wider Repertoire.** Choose 2 additional cantrips from the list (4 total). Your cantrips now last up to 1 hour or until you dismiss them.

3. **Effortless Casting.** Choose 2 additional cantrips from the list (6 total). Simple/touching cantrips (1 mana cost) are now free — they cost 0 mana.
