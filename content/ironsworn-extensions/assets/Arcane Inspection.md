# Arcane Inspection

*Asset · Ritual*

*Sense and interpret mana in a person, object, or area.*

A basic ritual taught early to Neophytes and Disciples of the Conclave, allowing them to sense and interpret mana in a person, object, or area — "detect magic," grounded in the color-coded manite system. It uses primarily Amber to read patterns, with Luminous to stabilize and Gray as a structural aid when needed.

## Abilities

1. **Arcane Inspection.** You can perform an Arcane Inspection to sense and interpret mana in a person, object, or area. Record the total difficulty by summing the difficulty factors and [[Cast Conclave Ritual|Cast Conclave Ritual]].
   *Moves: [[Cast Conclave Ritual]]*

2. **Practiced Inspection.** As above, but the spell difficulty is reduced by 2 (minimum 0) given mastery of the inspection skill.

3. **Master Inspection.** As above, but the spell difficulty is reduced by 4 instead of 2 (minimum 0).
