# Excluded Foes

Creatures from the Ironsworn and Delve bestiaries that do **not** exist in YRT, and the reason in each case. The pattern is consistent and worth reading as a set: almost every exclusion is something that requires a soul, a spirit, a returning dead, or a god that answers, and YRT has none of those. Where a workable substitute exists it is named, and most of those substitutes are now foes in their own right.

## Ironsworn

### Bonewalker

In YRT: does not exist as written — animated skeletons with the will to destroy life require a soul, and YRT has none. Replaced by Puppet-Bones: a handler-driven illegal skeletal construct operated remotely via an Amber command-link from up to two hundred paces away. Mindless when the handler quits. See the YRT Bestiary.

### Frostbound

In YRT: does not exist as written — cursed dead driven to seek warmth require a curse, and YRT has none. Replaced by the Coldhusk: a Luminous + Azure-saturated corpse in Luminous-rich permafrost, with a thermal-differential reflex to grasp anything warm that comes within reach. Does not pursue. Does not seek. Waits. See the YRT Bestiary.

### Haunt

In YRT: does not exist as written — a restless spirit tied to an unjust death requires a spirit, and YRT has none. Replaced by the Echo: an Amber imprint at the site of a violent death, burned onto porous material (stone, old wood, deep snow, standing water). A recording, not a person. Replays when disturbed. See the YRT Bestiary.

### Hollow

In YRT: does not exist as written — a dead Verdani's vengeance given self-assembling form requires soul persistence, and YRT has none. Replaced by the Mask-Risen (and its larger Greatmask variant): a Verdant construct walked by a living Verdani elder via an Amber-laced mask. Kill the mask or stop the walker, not the body. See the YRT Bestiary.

### Iron Revenant

In YRT: does not exist as written — an incorporeal force of vows given metal form requires a soul, and YRT has none. Replaced by the Vow-Walker: a Pura Ecclesia reliquary shell (Luminous armour, Azure joints, Crimson core, ceramic death-mask) operated by an Iron Warden handler via an Amber-encoded oath. Doctrinal necessity, not supernatural. See the YRT Bestiary.

### Primordial

In YRT: does not exist as written — vestigial spirits of forgotten gods require gods, and YRT has none. Replaced by the Locus: a walking mana-saturation point, dominated by a single colour, its body whatever local matter that colour wants to bind. Not sentient, not agentic, just very large and drifting toward higher flux. See the YRT Bestiary.

### Sodden

In YRT: does not exist as written — a lonely restless spirit of the drowned requires a spirit, and YRT has none. Replaced by the Bloater: an Azure-saturated corpse in a contaminated water source, with a reflexive spinal response to thermal and pressure stimuli. Grabs, holds, drowns. Not pursuing, not sad, just reactive. See the YRT Bestiary.

## Ironsworn: Delve

### Bog Rot

In YRT: does not exist as written — a preserved corpse that rises with intent, memory, and the ability to bargain requires a soul, and YRT has none. Replaced by the Mire-Form: a wild Verdant + Black construct using a preserved corpse as a plant-growth substrate. Not the dead person, but a plant using a dead body as a pot. See the YRT Bestiary.

### Bonehorde

In YRT: does not exist as written — the 'spiteful being at the heart' is a soul, and YRT has none. Replaced by the War-Pile: a pre-Fall AI construct with centuries-old standing orders, assembling structural mass (bones, scrap metal, masonry) around a Crimson-powered AI core. Reawakens when dragged into sunlight. See the YRT Bestiary.

### Circle of Stones

In YRT: does not exist as written — thinking stones that trade knowledge for memory require agency stones don't have. Replaced by the Whispering Stones: a leaking pre-Fall Amber communication relay whose decayed buffer inflicts neural-pattern interference on anyone inside the perimeter. Confusion, false recall, personality drift. See the YRT Bestiary.

### Glimmer

In YRT: does not exist as written — paths descending into the past or climbing into the future require time travel, and YRT has none. Replaced by the Wisp-Walker: a drifting Amber + Luminous particle cloud that lures travellers into blight zones by appearing to guide them. Not a person, not a guide — a feedback loop between the cloud and the observer's own brain. See the YRT Bestiary.

### Maelstrom

In YRT: does not exist as written — a sentient vortex that bargains with the drowning requires agency water doesn't have. Replaced by the Drowning-Eye: a wild mana vortex sustained by ruptured Azure and Luminous seeds from a shipwreck, the 'voices' an Amber imprint on suspended particulates. See the YRT Bestiary.

### Tempest

In YRT: does not exist as written — an intelligent storm that offers bargains requires agency weather doesn't have. Replaced by the Witherwind: a wild Luminous + Azure storm drifting along thermal gradients toward warmth, the 'voices' Amber imprints suspended in the Luminous lattice. See the YRT Bestiary.

### Thrall

In YRT: does not exist as written — possession by an undead spirit requires a spirit, and YRT has none. Replaced by the Carrier: a living host with a surgically implanted Amber compulsion-seed at the base of the skull, keyed to a trigger. The puppeteer is a living caster somewhere, not a ghost. See the YRT Bestiary.

### Wight

In YRT: does not exist as written — a corpse that retains reasoning and purpose beyond death requires soul persistence, and YRT has none. Replaced by the Tomb-Walker: a living person inside an illegal Gray + Azure life-support rig, sustained past natural death. Mortal, killable, and they know to the day how long their reservoir will last. See the YRT Bestiary.
